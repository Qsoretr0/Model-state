# Governance, Financing and Unresolved Design Questions

## Governance requirements
Any large-scale economic system needs clear legal authority, transparent budgets, independent oversight, public reporting, accessible complaints processes and meaningful participation.

## Financing principles
Do not present a benefit or investment programme as sustainable until its costs and funding sources have been modelled.

Potential sources to evaluate include:
- General taxation and improved tax administration.
- Reallocation of inefficient or poorly targeted spending.
- Returns from public or collectively owned productive assets.
- Royalties and resource revenues under transparent rules.
- Carefully structured public-private investment.
- Productivity growth and formalization, without assuming these automatically produce fiscal revenue.

Borrowing should be assessed against debt sustainability and the productive or social returns of the financed investment.

## Contribution and merit: unresolved design
The model explores contribution credits or recognition for innovation, caregiving, skills, essential work and environmental stewardship. These must remain separate from unconditional essentials.

Safeguards to design:
- Recognize invisible contributions such as parenting and caregiving.
- Prevent wealthy or influential actors from buying or manipulating status.
- Avoid permanent social classes or inherited rankings.
- Reward excellence without coercion.
- Provide transparent criteria, independent review and appeals.
- Protect personal data and allow people to opt out of public recognition.

## Further open questions
1. What exactly counts as the universal essential package?
2. What is the appropriate mix of cash and public services?
3. Which institutions should own and manage productive funds?
4. How should returns be distributed?
5. How can competition, entrepreneurship and private investment thrive?
6. How can development zones avoid becoming tax-haven enclaves?
7. How can local communities share in resource and infrastructure benefits?
8. What indicators should measure success beyond GDP?
9. How should the system respond to inflation, fiscal shocks and climate events?
10. Which proposals should be piloted first in Lesotho?

## Required next work

- Develop baseline economic and demographic data.
- Cost the essential package and alternative delivery models.
- Model fiscal and macroeconomic scenarios.
- Compare institutional case studies.
- Consult affected communities, businesses, workers and public institutions.
- Design small pilots with independent evaluation.
