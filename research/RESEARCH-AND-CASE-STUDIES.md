# Research Programme and Case Studies

## Purpose
Ground the Economic Model in reliable evidence and distinguish what is known from what is proposed.

## Research themes
- Poverty, inequality, household expenditure and social protection in Lesotho.
- Labour-market structure, informality, youth employment and skills.
- AI exposure by occupation and sector.
- Tax capacity, public finance and debt sustainability.
- Agriculture, food imports, processing and rural livelihoods.
- Water security, energy, transport and digital infrastructure.
- Mining value chains and environmental governance.
- Waste volumes, collection economics and circular industries.
- Education outcomes and lifelong learning.
- Cooperative, employee-ownership and public investment institutions.

## Case-study method
For each case, record:
1. The problem the policy addressed.
2. Institutional and economic context.
3. How the system was financed.
4. Measured outcomes and time horizon.
5. Distributional, social and environmental effects.
6. Implementation failures and trade-offs.
7. What may or may not transfer to Lesotho.

## Evidence standards
Prefer primary statistics, peer-reviewed research, official evaluations and reputable development institutions. Record publication dates, definitions and limitations. Do not treat correlation as proof of causation or promotional claims as independent evaluation.

## Working rule
Label content as **evidence**, **interpretation**, **proposal** or **open question** so readers can distinguish established facts from the project's own ideas.
