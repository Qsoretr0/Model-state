# Agriculture, Food Security and Rural Development

## Strategic role
Agriculture can support food security, rural incomes, jobs, exports and domestic processing. The aim is to build productive, resilient value chains rather than treat farming as an isolated activity.

## Areas to develop
- Farmer access to inputs, finance, extension and practical training.
- Irrigation, water harvesting and soil conservation.
- Storage, cold chains and transport.
- Processing, packaging and quality standards.
- Reliable market access and transparent pricing.
- Producer cooperatives and aggregation where useful.
- Research, appropriate mechanization and climate resilience.
- Linkages between farmers, processors, retailers and institutional buyers.

## Rural settlements and farm-worker housing
One proposal to evaluate is purpose-built container-home accommodation at farms for farm workers only, as a possible way to reduce commuting and housing pressure. This must meet safety, sanitation, privacy, dignity, labour and planning standards. It is a proposal for feasibility study, not a blanket housing policy.

## Food-system questions
- Which foods can be produced competitively in Lesotho?
- Where are imports likely to remain more efficient?
- Which value chains have credible demand?
- What infrastructure bottlenecks cause post-harvest losses?
- How can farmers receive fair prices while consumers retain affordability?
