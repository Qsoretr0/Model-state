# Water, Land and Sustainable Resource Management

## Principle
Water security, healthy soils and responsible resource use are foundations of human welfare and productive development.

## Water priorities to evaluate
- Protect catchments, wetlands and watersheds.
- Reduce leakage and improve water infrastructure maintenance.
- Expand appropriate storage, rainwater harvesting and reuse.
- Improve irrigation efficiency.
- Treat wastewater and prevent pollution.
- Plan for droughts, floods and climate variability.
- Ensure fair and reliable access for households, farms and industry.

## Integrated resource management
Water policy must connect with agriculture, energy, mining, settlements, ecosystems and industry. Decisions in one sector can create costs for others.

## Mining and natural resources
Resource extraction should be assessed against environmental impacts, water use, local value addition, employment quality, public revenue, rehabilitation obligations and long-term community benefit.

## Questions for research
- What are the major water losses and infrastructure constraints?
- Which investments provide the greatest social and economic return?
- How should water allocation be governed during scarcity?
- How can environmental monitoring and enforcement be strengthened?
