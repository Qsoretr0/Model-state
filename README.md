# Economic Model — An AI-Era Abundance and Survival Framework

## Purpose
This project develops an independent economic blueprint for helping societies secure essential needs, broaden ownership of productive wealth, develop national productive capacity and manage the economic transition created by AI and automation.

It begins from Lesotho's circumstances and opportunities, while considering principles that may be adaptable elsewhere.

## Central ambition
Move beyond an economy in which survival depends entirely on selling labour. Build an economy in which people can access essential foundations, participate in productive activity, gain ownership of productive assets and share fairly in gains from technology and natural resources.

This is a long-term proposal under development, not an adopted government policy or a fully costed implementation plan.

## Core pillars
1. Universal essentials and a secure basic-needs floor.
2. AI, automation and fair distribution of productivity gains.
3. Wider ownership of productive capital.
4. National economic planning and productive development.
5. Development zones, local experimentation and value chains.
6. Agriculture, food security and rural development.
7. Water, land, minerals and sustainable resource management.
8. Waste-to-value industries and circular production.
9. Education reform and human development.
10. Infrastructure, digital systems and public capacity.
11. Governance, rights, accountability and financing.

## Important separation
This is not MOTHEO, which is a separate privately owned digital platform project. It is also not the Autonomous Trading System. Any future digital tools that support this economic model are enabling infrastructure, not the economic model itself.

## How to read this repository
- **Core principles** describe the values and design constraints.
- **Proposals** describe possible policies or institutions that still need testing.
- **Research** should contain evidence, case studies and citations.
- **Open questions** identify unresolved choices.
- **Decisions** record what has been accepted, changed or rejected and why.

## Current status
Concept development. Major proposals require economic modelling, legal review, public consultation, feasibility analysis and pilot testing before implementation.
