# Productive Ownership and Shared Capital

## Purpose
Give more people a stake in the assets and enterprises that generate income and productivity, rather than relying exclusively on wages or transfers.

## Ownership pathways to evaluate
- Employee share ownership and profit-sharing.
- Worker and producer cooperatives.
- Community-owned enterprises and infrastructure.
- Accessible, regulated investment accounts.
- Long-term national or community development funds.
- Small-business ownership and entrepreneurship.
- Agricultural producer organizations and shared processing facilities.

## Principles
- Participation must be understandable and voluntary.
- Risks, fees and potential losses must be disclosed.
- Avoid promising guaranteed returns.
- Protect small investors from fraud and predatory products.
- Prevent political allocation of ownership from becoming patronage.
- Separate social protection from investment performance.

## AI-era relevance
Where AI and automation increase returns to capital, broader ownership can help more households benefit. This requires careful institutional design, competition policy, financial education and credible governance.

## Research needed
Compare international employee ownership, cooperative, sovereign wealth and community investment models. Assess which structures are suitable for Lesotho's legal, financial and institutional context.
